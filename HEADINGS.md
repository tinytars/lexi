# Every heading in this repository's documentation

308 headings in 36 documents, generated by
`plover-context`'s `infra/retrieval/gen_headings.py`. **This is the fallback, not the way in** —
[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) § *Finding an answer here* routes a question whose subject is known, and
this file is for the case where it is not: scan the headings for the words the question uses, then
open the one document.

Do not edit it by hand. It is regenerated from the documents it indexes, so an edit here is lost at
the next run and `verify-retrieval.sh` fails on the difference in the meantime.

Two things worth knowing before reading it. The nine documents over 15,000 B --
`apps/lexitar/DPGA.md`, `VAULT.md`, `AUTH.md`, `API.md`, `CORPUS.md`, `CREDENTIALS.md`,
`MEASUREMENT.md`, `LAWS.md` and `INGEST.md` -- carry most of the headings below, so a word
appearing once in this file most likely names a section of one of them rather than a document of
its own. And `apps/lexitar/` is where a figure or a procedure is, while the repository root is
where a claim and its limit are: a heading at the root is usually the beginning of an argument and
a heading under `apps/lexitar/` is usually the beginning of a mechanism.
